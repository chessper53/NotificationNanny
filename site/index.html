Something else on your mind? ↗
Is it really free?+

Yes. NotificationNanny is free and open source under the MIT License. There’s no subscription, paid tier or account to create.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?+

NotificationNanny uses macOS Accessibility to observe and move notification windows, and to pass actions and replies back to the real notification. This happens locally on your Mac.

Does it need Screen Recording?+

Only if you enable widgets in the custom Notification Center. The permission is used to display the real macOS widgets there.

Will it work with future macOS updates?+

The app supports macOS 14 and later. It works with private macOS internals, so an OS update can affect its behaviour. If something breaks, report an issue on GitHub.

NotificationNanny

Don't let macOS decide where your notifications go.

A small menu bar app that positions, scales, tints, and animates notification banners, globally or per app, on whichever display you want.

Download for macOS or install with Homebrew

Free and open source · macOS 14 or later · No telemetry

What it does

  • Any position, any display

    Nine anchor points plus a fine offset, with independent settings for each screen.

  • Size and colour

    Scale banners from 50% to 250%, tint the background or text, or swap in a custom styled banner.

  • Rules per app

    Put apps into named groups, each with its own position, display, scale, and tint.

  • Animations

    Keep the system animation, or pick slide, bounce, fade, scale and more.

  • Nothing missed

    Hold banners until your display wakes up, so nothing disappears while you are away.

  • Auto dismiss

    Close banners on a timer from 1 to 300 seconds, or leave them until you are ready.

Install

With Homebrew

brew tap chessper53/notificationnanny https://github.com/chessper53/NotificationNanny
brew install --cask notificationnanny

Updates are then one click from the settings panel.

Manually

  1. Download the latest zip and open it.
  2. Drag NotificationNanny.app into Applications and launch it.
  3. Click the bell icon in your menu bar and grant Accessibility permission.

macOS ties Accessibility permission to the exact build it was granted to, so you will need to grant it again after every update.

Privacy

No data is collected, transmitted, or stored outside your Mac. Accessibility permission is used only to observe and move notification windows on your own machine.

How it works

NotificationNanny watches Notification Center through the Accessibility API and moves or replaces each banner according to your rules. It relies on private macOS internals, so an OS update can change its behaviour. If something breaks, open an issue.

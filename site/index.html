Every corner of it is configurable

One menu-bar icon, one settings window. Drag a banner to where you want it and it stays there.

Position

Nine anchor points, pixel offsets, and independent placement per physical display. Drag a banner where you want it and it stays there.

Banner

Scale from 50% to 250%, tint the background, set the text colour, and choose from eight animations, including the system default.

Exceptions

Group apps and give each group its own position, display, banner style, scale, and tint. Everything else uses your defaults.

Presets

Save named presets covering position, scale, animation, tint, and per-app groups, and switch between whole setups at once.

General

Auto-dismiss on a timer, hold banners while your display is asleep, pause during Focus or screen sharing, launch at login. Export and restore your whole configuration as JSON.

Diagnostics

Copy your settings, system info, and recent activity into a bug report in one click, and read an activity log of the last 1,000 events when something looks off.

Help

Report a bug or request a feature from inside the app. Diagnostics are only attached when you file a report, and nothing is collected otherwise.

Install

Homebrew is the easiest path, and brew upgrade keeps it current.

$ brew tap chessper53/notificationnanny https://github.com/chessper53/NotificationNanny
$ brew install --cask notificationnanny

Prefer not to use Homebrew? Download the latest build directly from the Releases page and drag NotificationNanny.app to your Applications folder.

After installing, click the bell icon in your menu bar and grant Accessibility permission to get started.
